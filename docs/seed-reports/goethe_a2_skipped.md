## Notes on the alphabetical list

- Headwords wrapped over two rows (anbieten, nehmen, teilen, treffen) were joined into one entry.
- Rows with two headwords (Käse/Kasse, Reis/Reise, der See/die See, Seife/sein, erlauben/erlaubt sein, eigen/eigentlich, bekannt/Bekannte, mal/das Mal, verbieten/verdienen, an sein … seit) were split into separate entries.
- Some examples sit under a neighbouring headword (Ankunft under ankommen, Bewerbung under bewerben, Anmeldung under anmelden, Feier under feiern, Rundreise under Reisebüro); they are linked to the entry whose word they contain.
- 385 examples are identical to sentences of the A1 seed; they reuse its translations and annotations.
